# Community Code of Conduct

Folkscript welcomes people with different backgrounds, experiences, and levels of technical knowledge. Participation in this project should be respectful and free from harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and patient. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect different identities, cultures, abilities, and experiences.
- Accept corrections, take responsibility for mistakes, and respect requests to stop a behavior.
- Protect other people’s privacy and give credit for their work.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or demeaning language, sexual attention or content directed at participants, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not welcome. Publishing someone’s private information without permission is prohibited. These expectations also apply to retaliation against anyone who raises a concern.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to the project’s issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces maintained for Folkscript. Maintainers may edit or remove content, limit participation, or block participants when needed to protect the community. The response will reflect the seriousness and pattern of the behavior; maintainers may act immediately on threats or serious harassment.

Use GitHub’s **Report content** option to report abusive repository content. For a concern involving a maintainer or a matter that is unsafe to disclose publicly, use GitHub’s platform reporting tools. Do not publish private details in an issue to report an incident. Security vulnerabilities have a separate private reporting process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
